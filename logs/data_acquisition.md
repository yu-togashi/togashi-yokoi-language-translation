## Coverage (`analysis/tables/dictionary_coverage.{csv,md}`)
Generated by `code/experiments/dictionary_coverage.py`.

Key findings:
- **Folds are split by source-word frequency rank.** en-es: train source words have en ranks 2–5473,
  test source words 5474–7136. So with K ≤ 5000 *no* test word is inside the top-K, and with K=10000 only ~66% (en-es).
  → P@1 on the standard test fold can only be measured after extending the map to the full 200k vocab
  (otalign does this via Procrustes). P@1 from Γ's argmax on top-K alone is only measurable on the train fold.
  This may also be why otalign hard-codes `dict_fold = 'train'`.
- With 200k words, coverage is 100% for every pair and fold.
- One-to-many is common: en-es test has 1.98 translations per source word on average, 56% have >1.
- en-ru is the hardest: at K=20000 only 57% of en-ru test words have a gold translation in the ru top-20000.
